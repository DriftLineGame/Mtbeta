    On the mountain

    • Jump and drop speed. Before a big jump or drop a chip under your speed shows the speed it was built for, like Jump 52–56 or Drop 18–21. Green means you're on it. Blue, pedal. Orange, brake a little. Full assist handles it for you.
    • Drops. Wooden bridges run level while the hill falls away, and granite shelves end in a 5 to 10 foot drop. Hold your speed off the end and the rider lifts the front; come in slow and you land on the flat top.
    • Landings. In the air a gauge shows your bike against the landing. Match them and it turns green.
    • Junctions. Trails split and join. Signs and buttons show where each line goes. Your time follows the lines you ride.
    • Traverses. Highline, Long Haul and Sidewinder cut across the mountain. At a junction you can turn onto the trail they meet. Everywhere else they bridge over it, or drop over it, so you can keep going.
    • Career. Every trail and link in order, from green to double black. You start at the top of each one; reach the end and it's done. Each trail you finish unlocks a colour in the Garage.
    • Flips. In the air a Flip button appears above Pedal. Tap it for a backflip, or hold the stick forward while you tap it for a front flip. Keyboard: F (hold W for a front flip). Controller: ○ (stick forward for a front flip). You can also tap forward or back three times quickly. It always goes, but on a small jump you'll land halfway round and crash, so save it for the big ones.
    • E-bike. Pick it in the Garage. Pedalling on the e-bike boosts you at double power, shown by the battery on the left of the screen. A full battery gives 20 seconds of full boost. Coast or brake and it charges back up in 20 seconds. Run it flat and it has to charge fully before it boosts again.
    • Garage. Pick the trail bike or the downhill bike (more travel and takes bigger landings, but slower to pedal), then colours for the frame, fork, rims and the rider's kit.
    • Chairlift. Finish a run and choose Ride the chairlift, or roll into the loading area in the meadow. Two minutes to the top, drag to look around, skip any time.
    • Steering assist. Off, you steer everything. Light helps round berms. Full follows the trail and sets jump speed.
